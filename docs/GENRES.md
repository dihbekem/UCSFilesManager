## Changes from the first genre list

"Live" is no longer a main genre. It describes music played on real instruments, not a genre,
and is written as the keyword `Live Instruments`. "Hip Hop" under Hip Hop, "Pop" under Dance and
"Rock" under Live are no longer subgenres: only the main genre is set and Subgenre is left empty.
"Indian" is merged into South Asian, and "Brazilian", "Caribbean" and "Latin American" into
Latin & Caribbean. "Dance" is split into House, Techno, Trance & Hard Dance and Dance & EDM, and the
pop genres have their own main genre, Pop. "Classical" is now a main genre, and Cinematic and
Classical have subgenres. The table lists subgenres that were moved, renamed or added. The rest
keep their name but may sit under a new main genre.
